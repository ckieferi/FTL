About us

The Fintech Ladies are a network for women in the finance and tech industry. Headquartered in Berlin, the network has expanded to various cities all over Europe. We have regular events in many cities such as Berlin, Frankfurt, Vienna and Brussels.

Digitisation and innovation are transforming the financial sector fundamentally. We believe that fintech is the future of finance. Our network is for women working in fintech startups, banks, law firms, financial services and consulting.
Together, we want to learn and grow, we want to inspire and empower each other.

We want to shape the future of finance.

Events

The FTL Deep Dive is our regular community event. We partner with Banks and Fintech who invite us to their office and give an insightful impulse talk. So besides networking, every participant takes away new knowledge in addition to new connections!
